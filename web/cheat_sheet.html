The held layers: hold the button, then use the keys and knobs

A press of up to 1 s, touching nothing else, opens the button's pages (tap again: the next page); held, the layer shows after 0.35 s, or at once when you use a key or knob. Layer button + HOME: the layer stays open with the button let go (LOCK); any other button but PLAY, REC and the octave buttons closes it. On the SMK-37 Pro the 16 pads do what the FM-1's 16 white keys do.

Key aftertouch on and off

Hold GLO and press the lowest key

FM-1: GLO + F3. SMK-37 Pro: GLOBE + C3.

The screen says K-AT ON or K-AT OFF; K-AT stays in the top line, bright while it is on, dim while off. Kept with the settings.

  • FM-1: switches KEY TOUCH's aftertouch: VEL ↔ V+AT, and OFF ↔ AT.
  • SMK-37 Pro: turns KEY AT on and off: the keys' aftertouch, made from how hard they are struck and held (the keys have no pressure sensor).
  • SMK-37 Pro pads have their own aftertouch, always on: a light press plays, a harder squeeze is aftertouch. Set it on GLO > PAD AT and PAD AT 2.
  • SMK-37 Pro: the keys' and the pads' pressure glides to each new value over at least 10 ms (no steps on a resonant filter); a longer AT attack or release (GLO > AFTERTOUCH) works as set. MIDI pressure is unchanged.

Panic

From a computer or controller: CC 120 (all sound off) or CC 123 (all notes off); a DAW's panic button sends them. CC 121 resets the controllers.

Sounds: {{ENGINES}} engines

Pick an engine by picking one of its sounds, or with MENU > SCREEN > BROWSE = ALGORITHM. Its own values: EDIT 1, EDIT 2 (and EDIT 3 on MARE, PONY and RODEO).

  • HYBRID: three oscillators, two filters
  • PALOMINO: big early-1980s eight-voice poly
  • STAMPEDE: late-1970s poly, two channels
  • RODEO: punchy, brassy 1979-84 polys. Its sounds do not follow the key with the filter, like the originals; EDIT 3 KYBD adds tracking (0 to 100 %, 86 % its earlier tracking). PW0: the pulse's rest width, 46.8 % on BP SWEEP PAD, SOLO LEAD, PULSE LEAD, SYNTH BASS, SVF BASS and MORPH PLUCK, 49.5 % on the others, 53 % RODEO's earlier width. RES 124 to 127: the filter sings a sine at the cutoff, as the originals at full resonance
  • MARE: semi-modular, up to four VCOs, two stereo ladders; its warm, layered sounds play best in PARA (one filter, every key a VCO)
  • PONY: 1980s DCO poly with chorus. EDIT 3: RANGE 16' 8' 4', KYBD (how far the filter follows the keys), VCA ENV or GATE, CHAR. EDIT 1: WAVE OFF (the sub or noise alone). MODL: OTA by default, LADDR its earlier sound. CHAR: the original's quirks, CLEAN (none), CLASSIC (the default: those that match recordings of it), FULL (all); the editor has a box each (CUSTOM: another mix): STEPS (the cutoff knob in 128 audible steps; on in RESO SWEEP, FUNK BASS, DARK PLUCK), HISS (the chorus hisses while on), HPF (after the voices; 0 a +10 dB bass boost), LEVER LFO (the wheel's vibrato from the LFO), GATE 1 MS, DARK NOISE (~5 kHz), and in FULL only VCA CURVE (fast tails), LFO DELAY (free LFO, a waiting delay), COARSE CUT (~1.35 semitone steps), CPU TICK (envelopes and LFO every 4.2 ms). CHOR I 0.53 Hz, II 0.87 Hz as measured on the original (about 4 dB louder on); I+II is PONY's own. WAVE OFF, SUB and NOIS 0, RES 120 or more: the filter sings alone, in tune with KYBD 100 %; the wheel's vibrato up to ±4 semitones
  • FMX: six operators, any routing
  • FM6: six-operator FM, 32 algorithms
  • DIGITAL: four-operator FM pianos, bells
  • WAVE: wavetables, and your own
  • WSEQ: wave sequencing in tempo
  • VECTOR: four waves, a moving mix
  • ANALOG: virtual analog, filter models
  • ACID: acid bass, accents, slides
  • STRINGS: 1970s string machine ensemble
  • MACRO: chords, swarm, additive, speech
  • PHASE: phase distortion, bent sines
  • VPS: phaseshaping into formant sweeps
  • TERRAIN: an orbit over surfaces
  • PHYS: plucked strings, bells, drums
  • TRIO: 8-bit home computer chip
  • WHEEL: tonewheel organ, nine drawbars
  • VOICE: formant synthesis, sung vowels
  • NOISE: hiss to metallic tones
  • GRAIN: granular clouds, four sources
  • SAMPLE: playable instruments, four sources

Shaping the sound

MACROS (EDIT > MACROS)

BRITE the cutoff, TIMBR the engine's shape, MOTN vibrato and movement, SPACE reverb, delay and SPREAD. MIDI CC 16 to 19. HYBRID has its own M1 to M4 there instead.

MATRIX (LFO > MATRIX)

24 slots, each a cable: KNOB 1 SLOT, KNOB 2 SRC, KNOB 3 DST, KNOB 4 AMT. The page shows six at a time; the selected cable in large type, for example LFO1 > CUT +62, in its signal's colour. MATRIX 2 > CLEAR: turn right twice within 1.5 s to take every cable out; UNDO brings them back.

Patch bay (web editor)

Drag cables between 64 jacks to patch the same matrix, on every engine; the MATRIX tab shows them as a table. It is the editor's one matrix.

Web editor

The Sound tab opens with the sound's NAME, MODE and parts A and B; every save takes the NAME. The browser: MULTI, USER and the engines on the left, their sounds on the right. Work offline edits without an instrument, to a file. Click a heading to fold its card; Shift+click opens that one and folds the rest; Fold all / Open all on the Sound tab. ENV and ENV 2 show where the newest note you play is; the LFO graph has RATE, PHASE and FADE to drag and a button per wave. The Settings tab shows each device page with its place, such as TOUCH 2 · GLO 9/13.

FILTER and FILTER 2 (FX)

Nine models: SVF, LADDR, SK, DIODE, WARM, NOTCH, BP, HP, OTA. FILTER: MODL (ANALOG, WAVE, VECTOR, PONY), SLOPE 12, 24 or 48 dB (the 4-pole models: LADDR, OTA, DIODE), LINK. FILTER 2: MODL (OFF or a model), CUT, RES, ROUTE SER (in series), PAR (side by side), SPLIT (filter 1 left, filter 2 right). With LINK on, one cutoff sweeps both.

Voices and layers

EDIT > VOICE

VCE: POLY, MONO, LEG (legato), UNI (unison), UNI2, UNI4 (2 or 4 voices a note); GLD glide, GLMOD, PRIO. VOICE 2: ALLOC (ROT, REUSE, POLY2), DTUNE, PAN, VINTG. VOICE 3: SPRED, each voice its own place in the stereo field. 8 voices.

EDIT > LAYER

MODE: OFF, LAYER (a second sound on every note), SPLIT (the second sound below the split note), MULTI (two parts on two MIDI channels: CH, 2 by default). SOUND, SPLIT, LEVEL. LAYER 2: OCT, PAN, FILT, SWAP (edit the other sound).

Also on EDIT

MORPH between two versions of a sound, RANDOM by section, UNDO with COMPARE.

The arpeggiator in one move

Hold ARP and press a key

A mode key starts the arpeggiator in that mode; the mode already playing stops it. Or hold ARP 1 to 3 s, touch nothing, let go: start in UP, or stop. A short tap still opens the pages.

  • While ARP is held: KNOB 1 roll rate, KNOB 2 MODE, KNOB 3 HOLD, KNOB 4 RATE. The other modes (DNUP, UP+8, CONV, DIVG, PINKY, THUMB, WALK, CHORD, TURNG) are on KNOB 2 and the arpeggiator's page.

  • The top line shows ARP while it runs, ARPH with HOLD on, and HOLD for a key latch with MODE OFF.

Playing

  • LFO > BEND, BEND 2 (each sound): UP and DOWN range, VCF (filter bend), TARGT (ALL notes or the LEAD, the newest), which VCOs bend.
  • Aftertouch (channel and poly, from MIDI too): LFO > MOD AT> to VIB, CUT, SHP, WAH or TREM, and the matrix's AT sources. MW> does the same for the mod wheel.
  • KEY TOUCH (FM-1, GLO > TOUCH > KEYS): OFF (every key at 100), VEL, V+AT, AT (pressure alone). The 20 keys from F3 to C5 measure touch. Run TOUCH CAL once. TOUCH 2: the pressure's curve, offset, attack, release. ATOUT: send it as channel or poly aftertouch.
  • SMK-37 Pro: the middle 27 keys are the FM-1's; the five lowest and five highest add more notes. The pads play the 16 white keys F3 to G5, with velocity. GLO > PAD AT: a light press plays, a squeeze is aftertouch. KEY AT: aftertouch made from the keys' hit and hold. PAD VEL, AFTERTOUCH: the curves. The pitch wheel bends, the mod wheel is CC 1.
  • LEVER (FM-1): a key held, OCT- / OCT+ bend. GLO > TOUCH 3 > LEVER: LATCH (default), SPRNG, HOLD, OFF; LTIME. Out as pitch bend.
  • RIBBON (FM-1): a key held, turn SELECT. TOUCH 3 > RIB the range (up to 24 semitones); TOUCH 4: STEPS, SPRNG (LATCH, SPRNG, HOLD), DELAY, RETRN. Out as CC 16.
  • Sustain pedal: the SMK-37 Pro's PEDAL jack (plug it in before switching on), or CC 64 over MIDI. GLO > MIDI 3 > SUST = PATCH: the pedal holds no notes and only feeds the matrix.

The arpeggiator

Tap ARP. MODE: OFF, UP, DN, UPDN, RND, ORD, REPEAT, DNUP, UP+8, CONV, DIVG, PINKY, THUMB, WALK, CHORD, TURNG (a Turing machine), INCL (up and down, the ends twice). RATE, OCT, GATE.

Second page: SWG, PROB, HOLD, ORD. SPICE page: SPICE, DICE, LOOP (with TURNG: how often it changes, another pattern, its length).

HOLD with MODE OFF

The keys latch: the notes go on sounding and the next chord replaces them (HOME shows HOLD). It ends when the sound changes.

The step sequencer

  • Steps: hold SEQ, the white keys are the steps; patterns up to 64 steps (PATTERN > LEN), four pages of 16.
  • Locks: hold SEQ and a step, ALGORITHM picks a parameter, PRESETS its value on that step.
  • Ratchets x1 to x8 (KNOB 3; the step shows its count, such as C4 x8) and nudge (KNOB 4) on a held step; ARP held plays note repeats recorded as ratchets.
  • Fills: hold GLO + key 9 (while held) or key 10 (the next bar). A note's FILL setting (NOTES, OCT+ held) makes it play in fills only, or not in fills.
  • NOTES (tap SEQ): the piano roll. KNOB 1 walks the notes, KNOB 2 pitch, KNOB 3 length, KNOB 4 force (GHOST, SOFT, NORM, HARD, ACC), PRESETS zoom. The keys write the step; EDIT tapped deletes the note; OCT+ held: SLIDE, NUDGE, FILL, MOVE, COPY, DELETE.
  • REC records the notes and, with TOUCH 3 > MREC on, the bend, ribbon and pressure (MOTION lanes).

Saving

  • Autosave: what you are playing is kept across power-off.
  • User presets: SAVE > USER, 32 slots: KNOB 1 the slot, then KNOB 2 LOAD, KNOB 3 ERASE or KNOB 4 SAVE, each turned right twice. They keep everything new in 1.0. SMK-37 Pro: M01 to M16 after them hold multi sounds (parts A and B by name, the mode, B's settings).
  • Favourites: on SAVE > PRESETS, KNOB 4 right puts the sound at the top of the list (up to 16), left takes it out. KNOB 2 filters the list: ALL, one engine, USER.
  • Projects: no project page on the instrument; the autosave keeps the sound, the pattern and the settings. The editor's Backup tab saves everything on the instrument in one file: make one before every update.
  • SAVE > TOOLS: CLRSQ (clear the pattern), INIT (a plain sound), NEW (start again), each turned right twice.

MIDI

  • GLO > MIDI: IN CH OMNI or 1 to 16; OUT 1 to 16; BEND AUTO or 1 to 24 semitones (RPN 0 too); LOCAL ON / OFF.
  • GLO > MIDI 2: PROG program changes (and CC 0 banks) load sounds; SEND CC: the knobs send their CCs; BRTH: CC 2 breath plays the volume (VOL) and the aftertouch too (V+AT).
  • GLO > MIDI 3: CC A, CC B (any CC as a matrix source), SUST.
  • MPE: MENU > MIDI > MPE (CH 2-16): each note's own bend, pressure and CC 74.
  • CC 11 expression (the volume); CC 16 to 19 the four MACROS; CC 1 mod wheel; CC 64 sustain; CC 74 cutoff.
  • Clock: GLO > SYSTEM > SYNC INT, USB or TRS (follow a master); IN NOTES or CLOCK; MIDI KEYS or SEQ (what goes out). MENU > MIDI > CLOCK OUT (USB) sends it.
  • USB on both; the FM-1 also takes MIDI in on its TRS jack, the SMK-37 Pro sends MIDI out on its TRS jack.

Update and recovery

  • Install or update: open the site in Chrome or Edge (a computer) or Chrome on Android, with a USB data cable straight in. Press Install on FM-1 or Install on SMK-37 Pro. Make a backup first.
  • The page checks the package, the instrument restarts into its update mode and Stallion is written.
Do not unplug while it writes

If the write stops part way, the instrument stays in update mode: replug the cable and press Install again, and it finishes.

  • Stallion does not start: hold OCT- (OCTAVE-) alone while switching on: STALLION USB RESCUE, then install again.
  • Back to the official firmware: FM-1, Return to the official firmware on the installer page; SMK-37 Pro, the maker's own updater.
  • macOS: the MIDI port shows as Clipper or Hügelton Instruments - Clipper instead of Stallion? Unplug, delete that device in Audio MIDI Setup > MIDI Studio, plug in again.
  • MENU > SYS > FACTORY RESET erases your settings and saves (not the firmware): OCT+ to ask, OCT+ again within 5 s.

Settings worth knowing

  • MENU > SEQ > PARA ASSIGN (MARE PARA / PARA+): PLAYED (the default) the VCOs by the order the keys went down; LOW or HIGH always by pitch, the same voicing every time. PARA ROUND ROBIN ON (PLAYED only): each new key moves the VCOs on by one.
  • MENU > SEQ > PARA NOTE-OFF (MARE PARA / PARA+): KEEP (the default) a key let go keeps its VCOs on its note until the next key goes down, so a chord fades as a chord; HAND BACK gives them at once to the keys still held. The FM-1 always keeps.
  • The top line: the tempo (FM-1: after a metronome icon), the octave, ARP / ARPH / HOLD, SMK-37 Pro: the SOUNDS' B or A, K-AT (bright: the keys' aftertouch on), USB, the battery.
  • MENU > SYS > KNOB SPEED: values a click on the number dials, X1, X2 or X4 (the SMK-37 Pro starts at X2).
  • MENU > SCREEN > KNOB ACCEL: the faster you turn, the bigger the steps; OFF: one value a click.
  • MENU > LIGHTS (FM-1): LIGHTS every button's label from 1 (faint) to 8 (bright); KEYS lights the C keys, the white keys or all. On the SMK-37 Pro this section is PADS.
  • In MENU: SELECT the section, KNOB 1 to KNOB 4 its rows, PRESETS the row cursor, OCT+ OK, OCT- close.
  • MENU > SCREEN: COLOR, ZOOM (the touched value in large type), BROWSE.
  • GLO > TUNING: the system (EQUAL, JUST, PYTHA, MEANT, WERCK, KIRNB, RAST, BAYAT, SLEND, PELOG, 19, 24, 31-EDO, USER: a .scl from the editor), ROOT, A4 400 to 480 Hz.
  • GLO > SYSTEM: the fourth box turns through CPU, LATE and PEAK.
